What this build can prove

Event coverage, rolling analysis, readiness buffering, three grouped switches and custom rules and seeking behavior. It cannot prove content safety, frame-perfect audiovisual boundaries, or the false-skip target.

A 30-second delayed live stream cannot expose 180 seconds of future footage. This build analyzes seekable local files ahead in 30s batches. Initial waiting time depends on Google; the old 5s decision deadline is removed.
